# Fintech amplification questions

## objective
What is the specific financial output (income statement, cash flow, cap table, valuation)?
_Example:_ An 18-month projected income statement + post-round cap table.

## audience
For whom (internal founder, investor, bank, audit)?
_Example:_ Investor update for the seed round; medium sophistication.

## constraints
Data sample size, sources (central bank, exchange, papers, own data), regulation?
_Example:_ Own data for the last 12 months; everything in USD; central bank compliance.

## success_criteria
Which decision will it be used for (raise, hire, kill, double down)?
_Example:_ Decide whether to hire 5 salespeople or postpone for 6 months.
