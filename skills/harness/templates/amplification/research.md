# Research amplification questions

## objective
What is the specific research question that needs to be answered?
_Example:_ "How much does it cost on average to build and run an in-house AI team of 5 people in Brazil in 2026?"

## audience
Who will consume the report, and what will they decide with it?
_Example:_ CEO + CTO deciding build vs buy.

## constraints
Depth (1 page vs 30 pages), accepted sources (primary only?), deadline, language?
_Example:_ 8-12 pages, at least 70% primary sources, in 5 days, English.

## success_criteria
How will you know the report answered the question?
_Example:_ A clear conclusion with a number and a confidence interval; 3 scenarios.
