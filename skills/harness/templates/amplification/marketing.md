# Marketing amplification questions

## objective
What is the primary metric this will move (awareness, leads, conversion, retention, etc.) and by how much?
_Example:_ Increase landing page conversion by 30% in 60 days.

## audience
Who is the specific audience (age range, profession, funnel stage, geography)?
_Example:_ Brazilian SMBs with 10-50 employees in fashion retail.

## constraints
What are the budget, deadline, channels to use and what is off limits?
_Example:_ Budget $10k in 30 days, Meta and Google only, no influencer rentals.

## success_criteria
How will you know this campaign succeeded within 30 days?
_Example:_ CAC ≤ $10, 200 qualified leads, ROAS ≥ 3.
